Fixes for playback on a weak connection, and a tidier Settings.

- Songs no longer cut off part-way through when the network stalls
- A brief drop no longer ends the track — playback picks the file back up
- Screens say when they failed to load instead of looking empty
- A Deezer or Last.fm outage no longer reports your server as unreachable
- The equalizer has its own screen, so scrolling past it can't move a band
- Home shelf sources moved to Home; player controls moved to Appearance

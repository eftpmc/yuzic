Yuzic now has its own audio engine. Playback was rebuilt around an audio graph rather than a single player.

- Crossfade, with a gapless-aware mode
- Ten-band equalizer and replay gain
- Android Auto plays the quality you chose
- A unified Downloads screen, with progress and cancel
- A sleep timer that fades out rather than cutting
- Appearance settings for density, corners and glyph scale
- Screen reader labels on every control you can reach
